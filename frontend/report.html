Disease Description

Pituitary tumors are abnormal growths in the pituitary gland. Most pituitary tumors are noncancerous and can be treated through surgery, medications, or radiation therapy.

Symptoms

  • Headache
  • Vision Problems
  • Hormonal Changes
  • Fatigue

Precautions

  • Regular MRI Scans
  • Consult a Neurologist
  • Maintain Healthy Diet
  • Avoid Stress
  • Follow Prescribed Medicines

Treatment Suggestions

  • Medication Therapy
  • Radiation Therapy
  • Surgery (if required)
  • Periodic Medical Checkups
